Hagiography

Life History & Miracles

The Acharya Chosen by the Lord Himself

Manavala Mamunigal was born at Alwar Thirunagari (Thirukkurugur) in 1370 CE, in the sacred land of Nammazhwar. Named Azhagiya Manavala Perumal after the Lord, he was entrusted as a boy to Thiruvaimozhi Pillai (Srisailesa) of Thirukkolur, under whom he mastered the Tamil Veda and the Rahasya granthas.

His genius was the discipline of commentary (vyakhyanam). Expounding Nammazhwar's Thiruvaymozhi at Srirangam, he gave the tradition its definitive gloss — the Eedu — and across his 19 works he handed on, unbroken, the meanings received from his predecessors.

The Central Miracle

Srirangam Claims Its Acharya

When Mamunigal discoursed on the Thiruvaymozhi before Lord Ranganatha, the Lord Himself — speaking through the temple ritual — decreed that the ascetic seat be offered to him. The Lord would accept no other teacher.

"He is not one among men — he is Adisesha himself, Ramanuja born again. Let him be our acharya."

The Thanian decreed by the Lord that day is recited daily in Sri Vaishnava homes and temples to this very day.

Contributions

Works

Eedu 36000 Padi

  • The celebrated commentary on Nammazhwar's Thiruvaymozhi — rendered through his discourses at Srirangam.
  • Upadesa Ratthinamalai — a garland of doctrinal gems in devotional verse.
  • 19 works in all — stotras, granthas and commentaries guiding the sampradaya.
Sampradaya Preservation

Guardian of the Eedu Tradition

Carried forward the oral commentary lineage of Nampillai and Periyavachan Pillai, established the order of Prabandham recitation and study at Srirangam, and set the pattern of transmission that continues in the parampara to this day.

Philosophical Theme

The acharya as the Lord's own gift

In Mamunigal the tradition sees paramopakara — the Supreme Himself providing the teacher. Ranganatha's Thanian proclaims that the acharya is not chosen by men but given by the Lord, completing the doctrine of guru-prasada.

Theological commentary

Tradition commentary

Thanian of the Parampara

A Thanian is a verse of homage to an acharya. This one alone was decreed by Lord Ranganatha Himself, making Mamunigal the acharya of the Lord — and it is recited before every recitation of the Divya Prabandham.

Philosophical doctrine

Ramanuja Reborn

By hailing him as devoted to Yatheendra (Sri Ramanuja) and recipient of Srisailesa's grace, the verse confirms the tradition's view of Mamunigal as Sri Ramanuja's reappearance — the closing splendour of the classical guru parampara.
