Sthala Puranam & History

Origin legend & miracles

Rama gifted Vibhishana this image of Ranganatha as he returned to Lanka; set down at the Kaveri island it took root there — the self-manifested first among the 108, sung by eleven Azhwars and the seat of the Sri Vaishnava lineage.

Significance

The foremost of all 108 Divya Desams — the huge island temple of Lord Ranganatha reclining on Adisesha, sung by eleven of the twelve Azhwars.
