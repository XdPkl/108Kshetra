Guru Parampara

The Acharyas

The acharyas of the guru parampara — the teachers who received, preserved and expounded the Sri Vaishnava tradition after the Azhwars.

Purvacharyas — the early masters

Nathamuni

நாதமுனி

c. 823–951 CE

Recovered the Nalayira Divya Prabandham; first teacher of the lineage
◆

Uyyakondar

உய்யக்கொண்டார்

c. 10th century CE

Pupil of Nathamuni; strengthened the emerging tradition
◆

Manakkal Nambi

மணக்காள் நம்பி

c. 10th century CE

Guardian of the acharya line; teacher of Alavandar

Yamunacharya

யாமுநாச்சார்யா

c. 916–1041 CE

Alavandar — codified Visishtadvaita before Ramanuja
◆

Periya Nambi

பெரிய நம்பி

c. 11th century CE

Closest disciple of Alavandar; Ramanuja's first teacher
◆

Thirukachi Nambigal

திருக்கச்சி நம்பிகள்

c. 11th century CE

Kanchipuram saint who served the Lord of Varadharaja

The age of Ramanuja

◆

Ramanuja

ராமானுஜர்

1017–1137 CE

Bhashyakara — the systematiser of Visishtadvaita
◆

Koorathazhwan

கூரத்தாழ்வான்

c. 1020–1137 CE

Ramanuja's right hand; lost his eyes for the guru
◆

Mudaliyandan

முதலியாண்டான்

c. 11th century CE

First among Ramanuja's simhasanadhipathis
◆

Embar

எம்பார்

c. 11th century CE

Ramanuja's cousin-disciple, the lamp of the madam

Later acharyas

◆

Vedanta Desika

வேதாந்த தேசிகன்

1268–1369 CE

Kavitarkika Simham — lion among poets and logicians
◆

Pillai Lokacharya

பிள்ளை லோகாசாரியார்

c. 1205–1311 CE

Author of the eighteen Rahasya granthas
◆

Sri Manavala Mamunigal

ஸ்ரீ மணவாள மாமுனிகள்

1370–1443 CE

Accepted by the Lord of Srirangam as his own teacher
◆

P.B. Anna

பி.பி. அண்ணா

1848–1909 CE

Modern revivalist of the parampara
